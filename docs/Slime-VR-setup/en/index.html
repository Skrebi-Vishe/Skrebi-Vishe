1

Check that your trackers are suitable

This guide is only for trackers with temperature calibration

This guide is written for BMI160 and Wemos D1 Mini (ESP8266). The IMU is the sensor the tracker uses to know how it is rotated.

  1. If you don't have it yet, download and install SlimeVR Server. Keep the SteamVR driver option checked.
  2. Turn the tracker on, open SlimeVR and click the tracker's card. Find “IMU Sensor” and “Firmware Version”. If no card appears, see Troubleshooting.

Check the sensor type and version on every tracker.

Suitable: BMI160The “IMU Sensor” line shows BMI160. Carry on.
Not suitable: everything elseBMI270, BMI323, BNO085, BNO080, MPU6050, MPU9250, ICM‑20948, ICM‑45686, LSM6DSV, LSM6DSR. This guide will not work with them.

2

Get everything ready

Gather it all in advance so you are not distracted in the middle of a calibration

An extension is a second sensor on a wire, connected to the main tracker.

Calibration box

In step 4 the tracker has to lie flat on each of its six sides. A box with straight walls makes this precise and quick. Any small wooden box without a lid works: search for “unfinished wooden box” or “wooden craft box” on Amazon, Etsy or AliExpress, or look in a craft store (Michaels, Hobby Lobby, Hobbycraft and similar).

WidthDepthHeight
Outside203 mm (8″)103 mm (4″)60 mm (2.4″)
Inside182 mm (7.2″)91 mm (3.6″)53 mm (2.1″)

The exact size does not matter. What matters is that the tracker fits inside freely and all sides of the box are flat and at right angles to each other.

  1. Buy a wooden box without a lid, about 20 × 10 × 6 cm (8 × 4 × 2.5 in).
  2. Cut a slot for the USB cable in a long wall, almost down to the bottom, exactly opposite the tracker's connector. The cable should go out of the tracker straight into the slot without bending inside the box. Make the slot a little wider than the plug's strain relief, so the cable can be bent inside the slot and led out through the open side of the box when needed: you will need this when the box lies slot-down.
  3. Stick a piece of double-sided foam mounting tape in the centre of the bottom (for example 3M / Scotch or Gorilla mounting tape).
  4. Stick the tracker onto the tape exactly in the centre, USB connector towards the slot, edges parallel to the walls. The tape holds the tracker while you flip the box, so the tracker must sit firmly and not move.
  5. Label the sides with a pencil: X on the ends, Y on the long sides, Z on the bottom. This makes it easier to remember which sides the box has already been on.

A stop on the table

Put the box on a flat table and place or fix a stop next to it: a straight board, a block of wood or a thick book. Every time you flip the box, push it against the stop. That way it always stands in the same place at the same angle, and the calibration is more accurate. Step 4 shows what this looks like.

3D-printable cases: 8+2 set

If you need new cases, there is a ready-made set based on Hyperion, reworked for this guide: the cases have an opening for Type‑C, and the lids are labelled with the body part (chest, hip, left and right thigh, left and right shin, left and right foot). The set also includes cases with lids for extensions. The lid labels are in Russian.

3

Install firmware v0.5.4

Through the firmware website · exactly this version, not newer

You can skip this

If step 1 showed v0.5.4, skip this step and go to step 4. There is no need to reflash the same version.

Sensor rotationErase dataFlashing
Important

Why exactly v0.5.4. Temperature calibration for BMI160 exists in firmware v0.3.3 to v0.5.4. Starting with v0.6.2, BMI160 uses a new driver that does not have it: the console prints “Temperature calibration not supported for IMU BMI160”. v0.3.2 and older do not have it yet. That is why we install v0.5.4.

Flash through the website, not through SlimeVR. New versions of SlimeVR Server cannot install old firmware on these trackers. So we flash through a separate website in Chrome or Edge.

3.1 · Find the sensor rotation (IMU Rotation)

When flashing you have to say how the BMI160 module is rotated inside the case. If you get it wrong, the tracker will move the wrong way. Modules and builds differ, so look at your own tracker.

  1. Open the tracker case and find the purple module labelled BMI160.
  2. Hold the tracker the way it sits on your body: with the side that will face the ceiling pointing up.
  3. In the bottom row of the chart (labelled “Only for BMI160, v0.3.3 and higher”) find the picture where the module is rotated the same way: compare the pin rows, holes and small chips. The “Ceiling” arrow on the right shows where the ceiling is.
  4. The number in the label above that picture (0, 90, 180 or 270) is the value for the IMU Rotation (DEG) field. Write it down. If the tracker has an extension, find the rotation of its sensor the same way.
If the module does not match any picture

Some modules have a different layout. Pick the closest value and check after flashing: tilt the tracker forward, and its 3D model in SlimeVR should also tilt forward, not sideways or backwards. If it tilts the wrong way, reflash with another value.

3.2 · Erase the tracker's old data

The firmware website does not erase the tracker's memory, so old calibrations stay inside and can interfere with the new ones. To start from a clean slate:

  1. Connect the tracker via USB and open the Serial Console in SlimeVR.
  2. Type the command FRST and press Enter. The tracker erases its calibrations and saved Wi‑Fi, then restarts.
  3. Close SlimeVR Server completely, including its icon in the Windows tray next to the clock. While it is running it occupies the tracker's port, and the firmware website will fail to connect.

3.3 · Flash the tracker on the website

  1. Make sure SlimeVR Server is closed. Check all fields against the table and click Flash to device.

  3. Wait until flashing and Wi‑Fi setup are finished. Do not unplug the tracker during the process.
  4. Open SlimeVR and make sure the tracker appears and its info shows v0.5.4. Only then flash the next tracker.
Do I need “BMI160 Tempcal Debug”?

No, leave it off. With it, SlimeVR permanently shows a code like AXXYY instead of the normal temperature: A = 2 while calibration is running; XX = progress from 0 to 60; YY = temperature. A fully calibrated tracker shows 160YY.

The code stays forever, and to remove it you have to reflash the tracker and calibrate again. The normal temperature in SlimeVR and the check in step 6 are enough.

After calibration, leave the firmware alone

Reflashing invalidates the saved temperature calibration. If SlimeVR offers a firmware update, decline it. If you do have to reflash, repeat 3.2 and all calibrations again.

6-point calibration

Through the SlimeVR console · one tracker · ~5 minutes

The tracker learns what “level” looks like in each of six positions. It is taped inside the box: you flip the box and push it against the stop every time.

Two sensor calibrations

The sensor is calibrated in two ways: 6-point (this step) and temperature (step 5). The video below also shows spinning the tracker clockwise 10 times. You do not need to do that. It is a separate sensitivity calibration whose results have to be written into the firmware by hand.

Watch the video first: “Calibrating BMI160”, BANANKA Group channel

If the tracker has an extension

A tracker with an extension (a second sensor on a wire) is calibrated together with it: put a piece of double-sided tape on top of the main tracker and stick the extension onto it exactly centred and parallel to the case. Everything else is the same: the firmware calibrates both sensors by itself. After calibration simply peel the tape off. Switch on “With extension” above the 3D scene to see how it looks.

  1. Put the box with the tracker on the table the normal way (bottom down) and push it against the stop.
  2. Connect the tracker via USB through the slot in the box and open the Serial Console.
  3. Turn the box upside down (open side down) and push it against the stop again. The tape keeps the tracker inside.
  4. Press the tracker's restart button in SlimeVR or the reset button on the board. The tracker boots upside down and understands that it should start calibrating.

Tips
How to lay the cable
The cable must not pull, lift or shift the box: otherwise it does not sit flat and the calibration will be inaccurate. In most positions the cable simply comes out of the slot and lies loosely on the table. When the box lies slot-down, carefully bend the cable inside the slot and lead it out through the open side of the box. Leave some slack in the cable before each flip.
Why it matters how the tracker sits in the box
The firmware needs the tracker to have each of its six sides facing down while lying flat. After the first position the order does not matter, but it is easiest to copy the video. What matters is that the tracker is taped straight and parallel to the walls: then the sides of the box match the axes of the sensor inside the case and the calibration is accurate.
If nothing changes in the console
The tracker waits until it is moved and then left still again. If the “Recorded” line does not appear, check that the box is not moving and you are not holding it. If you accidentally put the box on the same side twice, the calibration will be inaccurate: turn it upside down, restart the tracker and start the calibration again.

5

Temperature calibration

Same tracker · 35+ minutes · noticeably reduces drift

As BMI160 warms up, its readings can slowly “float” even when the tracker is not moving. This is called drift. Temperature calibration helps reduce it. It starts by itself when the sensor is on and colder than 15 °C (59 °F), and saves by itself once it warms above 45 °C (113 °F). There are no buttons to press: you only need to cool the tracker properly and warm it up smoothly.

No cable needed

You can do this calibration without the USB cable: the tracker runs on its own battery and sends its temperature to SlimeVR over Wi‑Fi. The battery must be charged. You will check the result over the cable in step 6.

PreparationCalibrationSave messageTracker checklist

Preparation

  1. Turn on the temperature display in SlimeVR (as in step 2).
  2. Take the tracker's lid off in advance, before cooling. Without the lid, warm air reaches the sensor directly and the temperature follows the dryer more precisely: once the dryer is off, the case does not keep releasing heat and the tracker does not overheat. You cannot remove the lid later: the tracker has to stay still.
  3. Turn the tracker off and put it in the fridge or freezer for about 10 minutes. Only put the tracker in the fridge when it is off.

Calibration

  1. Take the tracker out, put it on a flat surface and turn it on. Check in SlimeVR that its temperature is below 15 °C (59 °F). If it is higher: turn it off, put it back in the fridge for a few more minutes and try again.
  2. Let the tracker warm up by itself to room temperature. Don't use the hair dryer yet.
  3. When the temperature stops rising, start warming it with the dryer to about 43 °C (109 °F). Hold the dryer at a distance and blow warm air from above, on a low setting, so the tracker warms up very smoothly. The airflow must not move or blow the tracker away: if it moves, no data is recorded at that moment. Do not reach 45 °C (113 °F), or the calibration will save too early.
  4. Turn the dryer off and let the tracker cool down to about 30 °C (86 °F). It should cool gradually; there is no need to hurry.
  5. Warm it with the dryer again just as smoothly, now to about 46 °C (115 °F). As soon as the tracker passes 45 °C (113 °F), the calibration saves by itself.
  6. Turn the dryer off and let the tracker cool. Put the lid back on.
If the temperature rises too much or the tracker does not cool down

Turn the dryer off and cool the tracker gently, without sudden changes: put something cold from the fridge next to it or, if it is cold outside, open a window a little. The tracker will cool down by itself, there is no need to hurry. Do not put it in the fridge in the middle of calibration: if the tracker gets colder than 15 °C (59 °F) again, the calibration starts over.

If the tracker is connected via USB and the Serial Console is open
Saved temperature calibration config (100.0%) for sensorId:0
DoneThe percentage shows how much of the range was recorded: the closer to 100%, the better. If the console also says “Calibration will resume from this checkpoint after reboot”, the calibration was not fully recorded.

Rules proven in practice

  • Don't rush. On average it takes about 35 minutes per tracker, but don't chase that time: the longer and smoother the heating, the better the result.
  • The tracker stays still the whole time. While it moves, no data is recorded.
  • Heating. For the last warm-up, aim for a sensor reading of about 46 °C (115 °F). Don't point hot air at one spot.
  • A tracker with an extension (a second sensor on a wire) is calibrated on its own, together with its extension. Two such trackers at once calibrate badly.
  • Two ordinary trackers without extensions can be calibrated at the same time if you warm them evenly.
Check the result

The sensor temperature passed 45 °C (113 °F). Let the tracker cool down. Tick “Temperature” in the tracker checklist and check the recording in step 6. Mark the whole step after all trackers.

7

Floor in the headset and in SteamVR

All trackers are calibrated

SlimeVR places your legs relative to the floor level. If the floor is wrong, your legs will sink into the floor or hang in the air, no matter how well the trackers are calibrated.

  1. In the headset itself, set up the play area and floor level again.
  2. Connect the headset to the computer and launch SteamVR.
  3. Put the headset on the floor and run the floor calibration (Room Setup) in SteamVR. Click the buttons with the mouse on the computer.
The main trick

While calibrating the floor, cover the proximity sensor with your finger on the inside of the headset, between the lenses. Otherwise the headset thinks it was taken off, goes to sleep and disconnects in the middle of the calibration.

9

Set up SlimeVR

Headset on, SteamVR running

Stay in SteamVR and launch SlimeVR. Go through the setup completely, in order, without skipping anything.

  1. Tracker assignment. In the SlimeVR side menu open “Tracker Assignment”. Pick a body part on the diagram and choose the matching tracker. To find out which tracker is which, shake it: it lights up in SlimeVR.
  2. Mounting of each tracker. Click the tracker and choose where it sits: Front, Back, Left or Right, exactly as it is worn on you. For example, a chest or hip tracker on your back is “Back”. Check trackers on the end of a wire (extensions) especially carefully.
  3. Mounting calibration. Open “Mounting Calibration” in the side menu and start “Automatic mounting”. Repeat the poses and actions the wizard shows.
  4. Body proportions. Open “Body Proportions” in the side menu. In SlimeVR v21.1.0 click “Manual Proportions”, then “Automatically fine tune proportions”. Follow the wizard.
  5. Do the mounting calibration and body proportions once more, ideally several times in a row.

Calibrate in VRChat

The first time you join

  1. Keep SlimeVR running and launch VRChat.
  2. Open “Tracking & IK” in the main settings menu. Stand straight, look ahead and click “Measure Height” next to “User Real Height”.
  3. In “Tracking & IK” set “Calibration Range” to 0.20 m. Turn off “Legacy Calibration” and “Use Legacy IK Solving” if your version has these switches. IK means calculating where the avatar's joints are.

Before every session

Only in this order

First, about the cold

Turn the trackers on only once they have warmed up to room temperature. Turned them on cold? Turn them off, let them warm up and turn them on again. Keep running trackers away from a cold floor and an open window.

  1. Launch SlimeVR and turn on all trackers.
  2. Put the trackers flat on the floor (or on a table) and leave them alone for at least 15 minutes, ideally 30. In this time they warm up and settle.
  3. Put on the trackers and the headset.
  4. Stand straight: feet shoulder-width apart, back straight, looking ahead. Press Full Reset.
  5. Do the mounting calibration in the skier pose (Mounting Calibration): bend your knees slightly and lean forward, as SlimeVR shows.
  6. If you have foot trackers, do the feet calibration (feet mounting reset).
  7. Start the game. In VRChat, do the full-body calibration, which binds the trackers to the avatar.
Why the trackers have to warm up first

When turned on below 15 °C (59 °F), the tracker starts a new temperature calibration. The saved data stays in memory, and the tracker uses it again after a restart in the warmth.

While playing

If your legs or body gradually “drift” to the side, stand straight and do a Yaw Reset. This is normal for IMU trackers. It is handy to assign a keyboard shortcut to it in the SlimeVR settings.

Troubleshooting

What to do if something is wrong

Firmware and connection

The computer does not see the tracker over USB

Check that the cable carries data and is plugged into the Wemos board, not the charging board. Install the CH340 (CH341) driver. Close programs that may hold the port: the Serial Console in SlimeVR, Cura, VS Code.

If nothing helps, check Windows itself. Unofficial stripped-down Windows builds (“Compact”, “Lite”, “Tiny” and similar) often lack components the USB driver needs to install correctly. In one case many different drivers were tried and none worked; it only started working after installing official, genuine Windows instead of a “Windows 10 Compact” build.

The tracker does not connect to Wi‑Fi

You need a 2.4 GHz network: the trackers do not support 5 GHz. WPA2 security, not WPA3. Channels 1–11. A network name with only Latin letters and digits is best. Don't use a guest network. If you changed the router or password, connect the tracker via USB and set Wi‑Fi again in SlimeVR or on the firmware website.

The tracker is on Wi‑Fi but does not appear in SlimeVR

Is only one copy of SlimeVR running? Allow SlimeVR in Windows Firewall for private and public networks (the SlimeVR folder has a firewall.bat file, run it as administrator). Set the network to “Private” and turn on network discovery. Turn off any VPN.

Sensor error in the console: “failed to initialize” or “No I2C devices found”

The wrong sensor or board is selected in the firmware, or the SDA and SCL wires (D2 and D1) are swapped, or the soldering is bad. Check the settings on the firmware website and the solder joints.

The console says “Temperature calibration not supported for IMU BMI160”

The tracker runs firmware newer than v0.5.4, which has no temperature calibration for BMI160. Install v0.5.4 (step 3) and calibrate the tracker again.

SteamVR does not see the trackers

Install SlimeVR with the official installer so the SteamVR driver is installed. In SteamVR enable the SlimeVR add-on: Settings → Startup / Shutdown → Manage Add-ons. In the SlimeVR settings enable trackers for SteamVR.

Tracking

A tracker drifts a lot

Check that after turning on the trackers lay still and warmed up, and that they were not turned on cold (see “Before every session”). Do a Full Reset. If the drift remains, check the tracker in TCAL Checker and repeat the temperature calibration if the result is bad.

A skeleton bone rotates the opposite way

Almost always the tracker's mounting (Front, Back, Left, Right) is wrong, or the tracker is not worn with its arrow up. If all is correct, check the IMU Rotation in the firmware (step 3.1). Then redo the mounting calibration.

Movements are too strong, or barely change when bending

Check that the tracker is assigned to the right body part and sits tightly. Thigh trackers must be above the knee, shin trackers on the shin, not on the foot. Then do the mounting calibration and body proportions several times.

The right and left sides behave differently

Compare the mounting of paired trackers: for example, one leg is set to “Front” and the other to a side. The setting must match how the tracker is actually worn. If everything matches and the difference remains, recalibrate the lagging tracker.

Legs cross when you sit down

During the mounting calibration keep your feet at least 10 cm (4 in) apart. Try fixing the thigh trackers slightly to the side or at different heights, reset and redo the body proportions.

Feet sink into the floor or slide

Recalibrate the floor (step 7) and check that your height is the same in SlimeVR and in the game. In the SlimeVR settings turn on “Skating correction” and “Floor clip”. Then redo the automatic calibration.

Feet are rotated wrong

After the mounting calibration, do the feet calibration. The foot tracker must lie flat, without tilting sideways.

The avatar in VRChat floats above the floor

Set up the play area and floor in the headset again, and check that the height matches in SlimeVR and VRChat. On Quest, turn off “Use in a lying position”.

Trackers were dropped or are old

After a hard knock the sensor inside may have shifted. Open the case and check that the module sits straight and nothing came unsoldered. Then calibrate the tracker again.

The tracker's LED is blinking

That's normal: the tracker shows its state with the number of blinks, for example that it is searching for Wi‑Fi or the server. If the tracker works fine in SlimeVR, there is nothing to do.

When to calibrate again

The 6-point and temperature calibrations are stored in the tracker and are done once. Repeat them only if:

  • the firmware was updated or reinstalled: erase the data with FRST first (step 3.2);
  • you replaced the sensor or moved an extension to another tracker;
  • the tracker took a hard knock and started drifting afterwards;
  • the calibration was done badly: the tracker was moved, rushed or calibrated before warming up.
